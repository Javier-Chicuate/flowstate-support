Flowstate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Applies to this version of Flowstate for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Your plan and settings

Flowstate stores your blocks, tasks, notes, projects, routines, templates, focus history, invitation email addresses and call links on your device. Settings are stored on the device too. The app and its widgets share data through an App Group. Flowstate does not provide its own account, server, analytics, advertising or tracking service, and does not sell your data. The developer does not receive your planner content through the app.

This version has no built-in iCloud sync between devices and no iPhone app blocking. System backups and storage providers you choose may keep copies of your data under their own settings and policies.

Calendar and Reminders

Access is optional and requires your permission. Calendar access lets Flowstate display event titles, times, calendar names and locations. Travel buffers use the presence of an event location; Flowstate does not request your device's location or calculate a route.

If you enable Calendar sync, Flowstate reads and writes a Flowstate calendar, including block titles, times, notes, task titles and call links. Changes and deletions can affect linked items. This calendar may belong to your default calendar account rather than local-only storage.

If you enable Reminders sync, Flowstate imports open reminders from selected lists and updates linked tasks and reminders, including titles, notes, due dates, completion and priority. You can choose a list for new tasks. Imported content is stored in Flowstate. Calendar and Reminders providers, including iCloud or other configured accounts, may sync this content to their servers and to people with whom you share calendars or lists.

Microphone and speech recognition

On iPhone and iPad, optional voice input uses microphone and Speech Recognition permission to turn speech into task text. Flowstate requests on-device recognition when the recognizer supports it. Otherwise, Apple's speech service may process audio on Apple's servers. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's privacy practices. Flowstate does not save audio recordings itself. Transcribed text becomes part of your locally stored tasks when you add it.

Sharing and exports

When you choose an email invitation, Flowstate passes recipients and block information to the system mail composer or mail app. This can include the title, time, notes, call link, open task titles and a calendar attachment containing invitees' email addresses. Your mail provider and recipients receive the content you send.

Backup exports contain planner records, including notes, invitation addresses, call links and focus history. Calendar exports contain block information. Export files are not encrypted by Flowstate. Keep them safe and share them only with intended recipients. Files saved to a cloud folder may be uploaded by that storage provider. On Mac, calendar invitations may also be written to temporary files. Opening a call link passes it to the app or service handling that link.

Widgets, notifications, Siri and Mac focus

Widgets, notifications and Live Activities can display block and task details, including on a locked device depending on your system settings. Spoken heads-ups can read block information aloud. Siri and Shortcuts can receive task input and return planner details through Apple's system features and any shortcut actions you configure.

On Mac, the optional focus feature reads running app names and identifiers to hide apps you select. Selected identifiers are kept locally; Flowstate does not send this app list to the developer.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can use the planner without granting Calendar, Reminders, microphone or speech access. Revoke permissions in your device's Settings or System Settings. Turn off sync and calendar display in Flowstate Settings to stop those features. Revoking access or switching off sync does not erase previously imported or exported records.

Planner records remain until you delete them. Use the app's item deletion controls and Settings > Data > Delete completed tasks where appropriate. Linked Calendar events can be deleted when you delete their Flowstate blocks while Calendar sync is on and permission remains granted. Linked reminders can be deleted when Reminders sync is on. Revoke the relevant permission before deleting local items if you want to preserve external copies.

There is no account to delete and no one-tap erase-all control in this version. Removing the app does not guarantee removal of shared containers, system backups, exported files, sent messages or records in Calendar and Reminders. Remove those copies separately using the relevant system, file or provider controls. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase data stored on your device or with your chosen providers.

Questions and policy changes

These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by Flowstate. If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are handled to respond to your request; do not send sensitive planner content. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by email, subject to those obligations.
